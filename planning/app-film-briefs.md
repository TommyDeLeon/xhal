## Production acceptance

For each film: record source revision/date; connect every claim and shot to evidence; document staging, time compression and synthetic data; validate licenses; render full-length horizontal and vertical outputs; watch both at real playback speed with sound and muted, including at phone size. Inspect beginnings, text holds, crops, transitions, ending, audio clipping and sync. Make one consolidated correction pass and a focused confirmation pass. Add captions and audio description where the accessibility requirements call for them; a general transcript alone does not satisfy every media criterion.

Keep app identity and truthful state visible. A rendering success is not evidence that the demonstrated app flow works. If a real app flow is blocked, deliver the storyboard and exact blocker without calling the film finished. No cloud render charges or paid asset purchases without authorization.
