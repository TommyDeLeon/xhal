# Brag plan — CodeLock and Tenant101 re-edit (26 September 2026)

Owner feedback on the first cut: not satisfied. Asked for a problem-first hook, crisp real UI with dead air cut, intentional pacing with zoom-ins, motion graphics (cursor highlights, arrows, lower thirds), proper sound design, burned-in captions and one clear call to action.

Tone: `default`, leaning polished — punchy and clean, never loud. Both films re-use the real captures already recorded (no new footage, nothing simulated). Landscape 1920×1080 and a separately composed vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps.

Shared visual system: ivory stock #F8F7F2, green #174C3C, ink #1D2924; Source Serif 4 for hook and CTA type, Geist for captions and labels; the lion mark on the CTA card. Captions are burned in as an ink pill with ivory Geist text, bottom-centre, held ≥0.3 s/word. Callouts are green: a pulse ring on every real click or tap (at recorded coordinates), a thin arrow with a label for the one number that matters, and a lower third naming the product. Honesty labels stay ("Sped up", "Demo data", "15 minutes later").
